The story so far

How it went, in the order it happened.

  1. The beginning

    It started small, the way good things do. A message, a coffee, a long walk home. Nobody planned for it to become this.

  2. The first time I knew

    Somewhere in those first months there was a quiet, ordinary moment when it stopped being a maybe.

  3. Learning each other

    The small things piled up: how you take your coffee, which songs you skip, what your silence means after a long day.

  4. The hard weeks

    Not every week was easy. We got through those too, and we came out better at talking to each other.

  5. Year two

    Less to prove and more to enjoy. The plans got bigger, the evenings got slower, and home started to mean a person.
